# How to read the pricing session notes

Moved from `docs/pricing/requirements.md`.

- They say what the department needs answered and what it decided, not how the page looks. The department
  asked for the indicators and their limits to be shown "in the most suitable and effective visual way", so
  the visualizations, grouping and order are yours to choose (`/mohammed:build-dashboard`, "Design").
- An answer in bold is the department's decision: propose it as a rule and cite the question.
- Limits, time windows (10 minutes, 24 hours, 7 days) and the days counted in a daily average are values the
  department may change: give each its own rule.
- Still open or unclear, so each becomes a proposed rule with a question for the department:
  1. Phone sales: is "assigned and answered" counted per assignment or per deal, and what if a deal is
     assigned more than once?
  2. Phone sales: does a paid deal that was never answered count as paid? The answer only says the numerator
     is usually part of the denominator.
  3. Phone sales: which payments make up the daily average? The days are settled: every day but Friday.
  4. Deals "by actions": are assigned deals broken down by the employee's last action?
  5. Employees: which status or stage means a deal is lost?
  6. General indicators: the basis of the ratios, as proposed in the table, or invoiced as a share of paid?
  7. General indicators: "invoiced" was answered "both" (an invoice issued and the appointment Completed):
     both required, or either?
  8. Limits: the values for the hour value and for the three phone-sales indicators are not set yet, though
     the three levels apply to them. Show those indicators without a level until they are.
  9. The on-screen name of the third level («قد يؤدي إلى الإيقاف» is not to be shown as written): propose
     one.
